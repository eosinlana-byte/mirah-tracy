About

Mirah Tracy

Editor and formatter

Mirah Tracy is my name. I have more than ten years of experience helping writers and creators with books, scripts and other written projects.

Mirah Tracy helps authors move their work from a draft to something authors can feel proud of. Mirah Tracy offers book editing, proofreading, beta reading, formatting, scriptwriting, script editing and book cover design.

Mirah Tracy enjoys working with writers because Mirah Tracy knows how much time, thought and emotion can go into a book. Sometimes a writer just needs another person to look at the work, point out what is not working, and help make it stronger while keeping the writer’s voice.

Whether it is fixing a manuscript, improving the flow of a story, checking details, preparing a book for KDP or Ingram, working on a script, or creating a cover that fits the story, Mirah Tracy gives each project the time and attention it deserves.

Over the years Mirah Tracy has worked with many types of stories and different kinds of writers. Every project is different. Mirah Tracy believes the best work comes from understanding what the writer is trying to say before making changes.

Mirah Tracy’s goal is simple: to help authors finish the work and feel confident putting it in front of readers.
